POWER FORMULA A: SUBJECT + IS / AM / ARE + ADJECTIVE

SubjectVerbExample
He / She / ItisShe is friendly.
IamI am helpful.
You / We / TheyareThey are cheerful.

POWER FORMULA B: SUBJECT + HAS / HAVE + ADJECTIVE + NOUN

SubjectVerbExample
He / She / IthasHe has short black hair.
I / You / We / TheyhaveThey have curly hair.

LEVEL 05: GRAMMAR REPAIR SHOP

Oh no! The sentences are broken. Read each correction to repair the grammar.

❌ Incorrect✅ CorrectWhy?
She have long hair.She has long hair.Use has with she.
He are friendly.He is friendly.Use is with he.
She is tall and kindness.She is tall and kind.Use adjective kind.
He has curly hairs.He has curly hair.Hair is generally uncountable here.
They is cheerful.They are cheerful.Use are with they.
She are helpful.She is helpful.Use is with she.
GRAMMAR CHEAT CODE
I → am / have
He, She, It → is / has
You, We, They → are / have
Say “curly hair”, not “curly hairs”.
